Privacy Policy for ECARUII

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Eye Controlled AR UI Interaction ("ECARUII") is developed and maintained by Alexander Neary.

This Privacy Policy explains how ECARUII accesses, processes, and handles information when you use the application.

Camera and Sensor Access

ECARUII uses device cameras and augmented-reality sensors to provide its core functionality.

The application may access:

  • Rear-facing camera images for augmented reality, environmental tracking, and object detection.
  • Front-facing camera images for facial-landmark detection and gaze estimation.
  • Facial-landmark and gaze-estimation values derived from camera images.
  • Object-detection and segmentation results derived from camera images.
  • Device pose, depth, and environmental information used to place and display augmented-reality content.

On-Device Computer Vision Processing

ECARUII's object-detection, segmentation, facial-landmark, and gaze-estimation pipelines process camera images locally on the user's device.

The developer does not receive or operate a server that stores users' camera images, facial-landmark information, gaze-estimation information, object-detection results, or segmentation results. Image data may exist temporarily in device memory while the application performs its computer-vision processing.

The developer does not sell these data or use them for advertising or user profiling.

Google Play Services for AR (ARCore)

ECARUII uses Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), a service provided by Google, to provide augmented-reality functionality.

According to Google's ARCore documentation, ARCore may collect information such as account or device identifiers, performance and diagnostic information, and information about ARCore API usage and application activity. Google uses this information for purposes including analytics, operation of ARCore, diagnostics, and improvement of augmented-reality experiences.

Google states that user data collected by ARCore on supported Android devices is encrypted while being transmitted.

Use of ARCore functionality is subject to Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Data Sharing

ECARUII does not transmit the application's locally generated camera images, gaze-estimation results, facial-landmark results, segmentation results, or object-detection results to servers operated by the developer.

Third-party platform services used by ECARUII, including Google Play Services for AR, may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies and data-handling practices.

Data Retention and Deletion

ECARUII does not provide user accounts and the developer does not maintain a server-side database containing users' camera images, gaze data, facial-landmark data, segmentation results, or object-detection results.

Because these data are not retained by the developer, there is no developer-held server-side copy of those data to delete. Information collected independently by Google Play Services for AR is subject to Google's own retention and deletion practices.

Security

ECARUII minimizes transmission of sensitive computer-vision data by performing its object-detection and gaze-processing operations locally on the device. Data handled independently by third-party services is subject to the security practices of those services.

Children's Privacy

ECARUII is not designed or directed specifically toward children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if ECARUII's functionality, data practices, or third-party services change. Any updated policy will be published at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
